Open our story ✨
Chapter I · The beginning

We weren't supposed to become “us”.

Kabhi socha bhi nahi tha ki ek online work-from-home job ke through mili hui ek ladki meri life mein itni important ban jayegi.

Pehle hum mile. Phir friends bane. Dheere-dheere baatein badhi, ek dusre ko samajhna shuru kiya… aur pata hi nahi chala ki kab tum meri life ka woh part ban gayi jiske bina ab main apni life imagine hi nahi karta. ❤️

“Kuch kahaniyan plan nahi hoti… bas ek din shuru ho jaati hain.”

Chapter II · That smile

Then I saw you smile.

Mujhe abhi bhi yaad hai jab maine tumhe pehli baar smile karte hue dekha tha. Pata nahi us smile mein kya tha… but us waqt meri heartbeat sach mein badh gayi thi.

Shayad wahi pehli baar tha jab mujhe laga tha — “ye ladki mere liye thodi si special hai.” ✨

Aur aaj bhi tumhe smile karte hue dekhna meri favourite cheezon mein se ek hai.

Chapter III · Why you matter

Your happiness matters to me.

Tum jab genuinely khush hoti ho na, mujhe woh bahut achha lagta hai. Main chahta hoon tum hamesha waise hi khush raho.

Aur agar kabhi life mein koi aisa time aaye jab tumhari smile thodi kam ho jaaye, toh bas itna yaad rakhna — Dudu hamesha tumhare saath hai. 🤍

Chapter IV · I cared

I never wanted anything in return.

Jab hum mile the, tum apne past ki wajah se already bahut kuch face kar rahi thi aur tum relationship mein bilkul nahi aana chahti thi.

Us waqt maine tumhare liye jo bhi kiya, maine kabhi ye sochkar nahi kiya ki main tumhare liye kuch kar raha hoon ya tumhe kabhi iska return dena hai.

“Maine bas wahi kiya jo mujhe tumhare liye sahi laga.
Because I cared about you.” ❤️

Aur aaj jab tum mujhe apni family ke baad apni first priority samajhti ho… honestly, mere liye usse zyada special feeling shayad kuch nahi ho sakti. 🥺

Chapter V · The little things

I don't love only the easy parts.

😊 Tumhari hasi
❤️ Tumhara care karna
😂 Tumhari silly baatein
😤 Tumhara gussa
🌙 Tumhare moods
🥹 Tumhara mujhe “Dudu” kehna

Mujhe tumhari sirf achhi cheezein nahi pasand. Mujhe tumhari woh saari little things pasand hain jo tumhe tum banati hain.

“I don't want perfect. I want you.” 🫶

Chapter VI · The future

I don't see you only in my present.

Jaan, future ko lekar ek baat main tumhe aaj, tumhare birthday par clearly kehna chahta hoon.

Main tumhe sirf aaj ke liye nahi dekhta.
Main tumhare saath aage ki life imagine karta hoon. 💍

Main genuinely chahta hoon ki hum isi tarah saath aage badhte rahein — ek dusre ko samajhte hue, support karte hue, apni mistakes se seekhte hue aur life ke har phase ko saath face karte hue.

“Main tumhe apni wife maanta hoon.” 💍

Ye mere liye sirf ek word nahi hai. Ye woh future hai jiske liye main kaam karna chahta hoon, khud ko better banana chahta hoon aur apni life ko stable banana chahta hoon. 🌟

Mujhe pata hai wo din aaj nahi hai. Abhi mujhe bahut kuch achieve karna hai. 💪

Chapter VII · One day

The day I dream about.

“Bubu, ab ye sirf humara sapna nahi hai.
Ab ye humari reality hai.” 🥹❤️

Us din main peeche mudkar humari poori journey yaad karunga — ek online job se shuru hui friendship, tumhari wo first smile, tumhara difficult phase, humari endless conversations, fights, laughter aur ye saari choti-choti memories.

Aur shayad tab mujhe sabse zyada khushi is baat ki hogi ki maine jis ladki ko ek din bas screen par dekha tha, aaj wahi ladki mere saamne meri poori zindagi bankar khadi hai. 🥺❤️

Bas tab tak mera haath pakad ke mere saath chalti rehna. 🤝

Chapter VIII · My promise

Not just every birthday.

Hum perfect nahi hain. But I want us to keep choosing each other.

Har birthday par nahi…
Har din. ❤️
